# mlkbar Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

mlkbar is a baby tracker for logging feeds, pumping, naps, nappies, food, allergens and growth.
It is built so that **your data stays with you**. The developer never receives, sees or stores
anything you log.

## The short version

- No accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- Your data is stored either in **your own iCloud account** or on **a server you run yourself**.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

## What the app stores

The information you choose to enter, for example:

- Your child's name, date of birth and sex (used for growth percentiles)
- Feeds, pumping, breastfeeds, naps, nappies, food, notes and allergen tries
- Weight, length and head circumference
- App settings such as quick feed amount and button order

## Where it is stored

**iCloud (default)**
Your data is kept in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. Only you, and anyone you
choose to share with, can access it. The developer has no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud
data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**Self-hosted**
Your data is sent only to the server address you enter, a server you or your household run. It is
not sent anywhere else. The person running that server is responsible for it.

**On your device**
A copy is kept on your iPhone so the app works offline, and a small summary is shared with the
mlkbar widgets on your device. If you use a self-hosted server, your sign-in token is stored in the
iOS Keychain. These stay on your device.

## Sharing with a partner

If you use iCloud and invite a partner, the people you invite can see and add to your data through
iCloud sharing. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings.

## Siri, widgets and Live Activities

Siri shortcuts, widgets and Live Activities read and write the same data on your device. mlkbar does
not send this information to the developer.

## Crash reports

mlkbar includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools. If you have chosen to share analytics with
app developers in iOS Settings, or you are a TestFlight tester, Apple may provide anonymous crash
reports to the developer. These do not include the data you log.

## Children's privacy

mlkbar is used by parents and carers to record information about their child. The app does not
collect this information. It stays in your iCloud or on your own server, as described above.

## Your control over your data

- **Export:** Settings → Export Data creates a copy of everything as a file. Settings → PDF Report
  creates a printable summary.
- **Remove from this phone:** Settings → Stop Using iCloud Here (or Log Out when self-hosted).
- **Delete iCloud data:** delete entries in the app, or remove all mlkbar data in iOS Settings →
  [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → mlkbar.
- **Delete self-hosted data:** delete it from your server.
- Deleting the app removes the data stored on your phone.
